# KYNEX — 2-Minute Video Pitch Script (v2, timing-tight)

> **Total: ~120s · 9 beats.** Format: `[MM:SS] BEAT — (VISUAL) narration`.
> Word count is the script: ~300 words ≈ 120s at a natural pitch pace. If you read it faster, *add pauses*, don't add words.

---

**[0:00] THE HOOK — (screen: student buried in 6 app windows, cursor spinning)**
> "Six apps. Six subscriptions. And still the same blind guess every night: *what do I actually study?*"
> *(beat — 1s of silence on the spinning cursor)*

**[0:06] THE TURN — (hard cut: KYNEX dashboard, single card glowing — "NEXT MOVE")**
> "This is KYNEX. It doesn't ask what you want to learn. It already knows — because it watched you practice."

**[0:13] THE LOOP — (animated: KNOW → UNDERSTAND → ACT → MASTER → ADVANCE)**
> "Everything here is one evidence loop. Your Vault feeds the AI Professor. The Professor feeds practice. Practice feeds mastery analytics — and analytics picks tomorrow's highest-impact target."

**[0:26] DIFFERENTIATOR 1: NO HALLUCINATIONS — (screen: AI Professor answering, citations visible)**
> "And it can't invent facts. The Professor only teaches from *your own* material — a deterministic gate blocks off-scope questions **before** the AI is ever called. When your Vault doesn't contain the answer, KYNEX says so."

**[0:44] DIFFERENTIATOR 2: EVIDENCE MATH — (screen: Twin page, eight dimensions + calibration)**
> "Mastery isn't a vibe here. Eight dimensions, confidence calibration, memory-decay tracking — all computed from real practice evidence, with the spaced-repetition math running server-side where it can't be gamed."

**[1:00] DIFFERENTIATOR 3: ZERO-TRUST — (screen: test suite running green)**
> "And it's built zero-trust. Every request is re-verified against the caller server-side — proven by an adversarial test suite whose only job is to break tenant isolation. Three hundred twenty-five tests. All green."

**[1:16] GLOBAL — (screen: language switcher → Arabic RTL view)**
> "Ten languages. Native right-to-left. Because the next billion students don't study in English."

**[1:27] IMPACT — (screen: student closes laptop, relieved; Twin card: "verified")**
> "Six tools guessed. KYNEX knows. Students stop asking *what* to study — and start finishing what matters."

**[1:40] CLOSE + CTA — (logo + live-demo URL, hold 3s)**
> "KYNEX — the Academic Intelligence OS. Try the live demo at the link below. Stop guessing what to study."
> *(end card: URL + team names, no music cut-off — fade by 2:00)*

---

### Recording notes
1. **The hook is unforgiving:** lines at 0:00–0:05 must be spoken *over* the chaotic screen — no logo, no title card first. Title cards at the start measurably lose hackathon judges.
2. **Screen-recording order = script order:** record dashboard, Professor chat, Twin, test suite, RTL switch in exactly that order so the edit needs no fancy transitions.
3. **Never say:** "guaranteed results," "replaces teachers," "AGI." Every claim in this script maps to a shipped feature — keep it that way.
4. **If the edit runs long:** trim beat [1:16] (Global) first — it's the most expendable; never trim the hook, the differentiators, or the close.
5. **Captions:** burn in subtitles — a large share of judges watch muted.
